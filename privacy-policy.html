Privacy Policy

How Jewel Frontier collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Jewel Frontier (“we”, “us”, “our”) publishes www.jewelfrontier.com, an independent guide to watches as jewellery. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it and the choices available to you.

Information you give us

When you contact us, we receive your name, email address, chosen topic and message. When you subscribe to The Frontier Letter, we receive your email address. We use this information solely to reply or to send the newsletter. We never request payment details, as we sell nothing.

Information collected automatically

We automatically receive technical data such as IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring page, pages visited and the time of your visit. The jewel counter and wrist sizer run in your browser; your inputs, including wrist measurements, are not sent to us. See our Cookie Policy for cookie details.

How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and corrections.
  • To send the newsletter to subscribers.
  • To understand which guides are valuable and improve them.
  • To protect the website against spam and misuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to measure how visitors use the website. It uses cookies to collect information such as pages viewed and session length, processed by Google under its own policies; see How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising

This website may display ads served by Google and other third-party vendors. These vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info/choices.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate the website, such as hosting and email delivery, or where the law requires.

Retention

Messages are kept only as long as needed to respond. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. Analytics data follows our Google Analytics retention settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR and CCPA, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information. Contact us to make a request. Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal data.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including HTTPS encryption. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows the current version.
