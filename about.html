Our story

About Jewel Frontier

An independent guide exploring the meeting point of watchmaking and jewellery, written for anyone who loves beautiful, well-made objects.

How it began

A grandmother’s cocktail watch

Jewel Frontier began with a tiny gold cocktail watch, its dial no bigger than a fingernail, framed by a ring of small stones. Nobody knew whether the stones were diamonds or glass, whether the case was solid gold, or why the movement was stamped with a number followed by the word “jewels”.

Finding out meant learning about metals, gems and movements all at once. This website gathers that knowledge in one place, clearly and without hype.

Independent

We do not sell watches or jewellery and are not affiliated with any brand or retailer.

Materials first

We focus on what things are made of and how they are crafted, not on logos or prices.

Clear and honest

We explain technical terms simply and say plainly when something is uncertain.

How we work

Research you can rely on

Our guides draw on gemmological and horological references, technical information published by material suppliers and conversations with watchmakers and jewellers. We do not authenticate, value or appraise items.
