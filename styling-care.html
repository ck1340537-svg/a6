Wear it well

Styling and caring for precious watches

Practical advice on wearing a watch as part of your jewellery wardrobe and keeping precious metals, gems and crystals at their best.

Day to evening

One watch, many occasions

A well-chosen watch moves easily through the day. A slim case on a leather strap suits office wear; swap to a metal bracelet or add fine bangles for the evening.

  • Daytime: steel or two-tone, simple dial, leather or bracelet.
  • Evening: gold or gem-set, smaller case, minimal extra jewellery.
  • Formal events: a thin dress watch that slips under a cuff.
Tailoring

Watches with formal wear

With a suit, aim for a watch that sits neatly under the shirt cuff. Thin cases, simple dials and black or brown leather straps are classic choices. Match strap colour to shoes and belt for a polished finish.

Cufflinks, tie bars and rings look best in the same metal family as the watch case.

Care by material

Cleaning cheat sheet

Gold & platinumSoft cloth after wearing; specialist polishing occasionallyAbrasive cloths, household cleaners
Stainless steelSoft brush and lukewarm water if water resistant; dry thoroughlyChlorine, harsh chemicals
Diamonds & gem settingsSoft brush to remove residue; check settings regularlyKnocks, ultrasonic cleaning at home
Mother-of-pearl dialsProtected under the crystal; keep the case sealedOpening the case yourself
Leather strapsAir after wearing; rotate strapsWater, perfume, direct heat
Servicing

When to see a specialist

Mechanical movements generally benefit from a full service every few years, as recommended by the maker. Gem-set watches also need their settings inspected and cleaned by someone experienced with jewellery.

  • Stones that move, click or look misaligned.
  • Moisture or fog under the crystal.
  • A crown that feels loose or gritty.
  • Timekeeping that changes suddenly.

Always follow the specific guidance for your watch.

Heirlooms

Passing a watch down

Precious watches often become family heirlooms. Keep any original papers and service records together with the watch, note its history, and have it checked before handing it on so it arrives ready to wear.

A short handwritten note about where and when the watch was worn turns a beautiful object into a treasured story.
